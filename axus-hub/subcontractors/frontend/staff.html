Subcontractor onboarding — how it works

Onboarding takes a subcontractor from first contact to an approved, compliant vendor. Staff drive it from this console; the subcontractor completes their part through a secure link we email them.

The process, step by step

  1. Add the subcontractor. Go to Directory → + Add Subcontractor. Company legal name and email are required; add contact, address, and services if you have them. The record starts in invited.
  2. Send the invite. Open the subcontractor and click ✉ Send / resend invite. They get a secure, single-use onboarding link by email. You can resend it anytime.
  3. Subcontractor completes onboarding. Through that link they confirm their company profile and submit the three required items:
    • W-9 — tax form (uploaded)
    • COI — Certificate of Insurance (uploaded)
    • Subcontractor Agreement — signed electronically via Axus eSign
    Status moves invited → onboarding → pending review.
  4. Review the documents. Open the subcontractor → Documents, then Approve or Reject each one.
    • Approving a COI asks for its expiration date — that drives renewal tracking.
    • The Agreement is completed in eSign; on signature it records the signed date and a 24-month renewal date automatically.
    • Rejecting sends it back to the subcontractor for correction (add a reason).
  5. Approve the vendor. When W-9, COI, and Agreement are all approved, overall Compliance becomes compliant. Click Approve to set the vendor approved. Other actions: Request correction, Hold, Mark inactive.
  6. Stay compliant (ongoing). The Dashboard surfaces what needs attention — COIs expiring in 30 days or expired, agreements due or overdue (24-month renewal), missing W-9s, and non-compliant vendors. Click any tile to jump to that filtered list.

Status reference

invited added, not yet started · onboarding subcontractor is filling it out · pending review waiting on staff · approved compliant & active · missing documents · expiring soon COI/agreement nearing renewal · non compliant · on hold / inactive

Overall compliance is computed from the three items: W-9, COI (with its expiration), and the signed Agreement.

Good to know

  • Every change is recorded in each subcontractor's Activity; use Notes (internal) for staff-only remarks the subcontractor never sees.
  • Buttons you see are gated by your role (invite, approve, review documents, override compliance, deactivate) — if an action is missing, your role doesn't include it.
  • Nothing is emailed to the subcontractor except the invite and eSign agreement; reviews and notes stay internal.
